About the author

Francesco Durazzo is a journalist and a technologist. Born in São Paulo in the late 70s, he was about the age of his first novel's protagonist in 1994 and hung out in the same places as its characters. He was a fairly active figure in that underground scene, as a fanzine editor, the organizer of a writers' collective and a writer for all sorts of publications. Shortly after that period, he moved to the United States, then to the United Kingdom. The novel is as much an exercise in nostalgia as it is the building of a mystery. He lives in London with his wife and two cats.
