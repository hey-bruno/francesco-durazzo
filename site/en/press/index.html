Synopsis

Short

São Paulo, 1994. Odete is seventeen, scared she’ll fail the university entrance exam, and has hardly any customers at her uncle’s rubber-stamp shop in the Rock Galleria. When the leader of a hardcore band swears his demo tape has been tampered with, she decides to investigate, with friends who have more free time than qualifications. Long

São Paulo, October 1994. Two years after impeaching a president, Brazil buried Senna, won the World Cup, and changed its currency. Prices stopped going up, for now.

Odete is seventeen, has a black trench coat, and is scared she’ll fail the harsh entrance exam for her chosen university. She’s lived with her grandmother since her parents moved to a gated community outside the city without her. In the afternoons she minds her uncle’s rubber-stamp shop in a building officially called the Grand Galleria and known to everyone as the Rock Galleria, where São Paulo’s underground scene buys records, kills time, and turns every rumor into a conspiracy theory. Customers hardly ever show up, so she has time for what she does best: sticking her nose where it doesn’t belong and tracking every story to its source.

When the leader of a hardcore band shows up complaining that his demo tape has been tampered with, Odete decides to investigate. As the case gets hairier, she recruits a handful of people from the scene to chase it down: friends with more free time than qualifications, and a girl who complicates everything.

As the story gets out, the press blames the devil, extraterrestrials, a real-estate scam, or mass hysteria. A pastor goes on TV to call the building the maternity ward of the Apocalypse, and anyone who listens to metal, punk, or goth rock becomes a suspect.

With exams coming up and her family telling her to drop everything, Odete has to choose between the future expected of her and a mystery that was never any of her business.

Author

Short bio

Francesco Durazzo is a journalist and a technologist. Born in São Paulo in the late ’70s, he was about the age of his first novel’s protagonist in 1994 and hung out in the same places as its characters. He was a fanzine editor, the organizer of a writers’ collective, and a contributor to all sorts of publications in the city’s underground scene. Shortly after, he moved to the United States, then to the United Kingdom.
